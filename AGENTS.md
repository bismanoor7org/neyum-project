# Product north star — beat [fiji.travel](https://www.fiji.travel/)

**Do NOT copy Tourism Fiji.** Use it only as the bar to clear. Every page must feel clearly more premium, more useful, and more conversion-ready.

## Positioning
| fiji.travel (official) | This project (luxury) |
|---|---|
| Mass tourism, “happiness” brand | Bespoke luxury, curated journeys |
| Generic carousel & lists | Editorial photography, handpicked cards, story-led sections |
| Browse-only, no hero booking | Instant “Plan Your Journey” + concierge trust |
| Government template feel | Magazine-grade UI — Playfair + Inter, cream/navy/gold |

## Non‑negotiables
1. **Unique voice** — never reuse their headline “Where happiness comes naturally” as primary hero copy.
2. **Fiji-verified imagery** — editorial quality, no generic stock, no duplicates where avoidable.
3. **Conversion path** — hero booking, clear CTAs, trust bar, guides, deals, FAQ, newsletter on every relevant flow.
4. **Performance & polish** — no glitchy hover zoom on gradient-fade cards; subtle shadows and motion only.
5. **Scope discipline** — match reference screenshots when given; otherwise innovate within the design system.

## Home page must win on
- Cinematic hero + glass feature bar + live booking widget
- “Discover Fiji Your Way” categories (6 luxury intents)
- Handpicked tours, Fiji Story video, Plan with confidence, Exclusive deals
- Social proof + FAQ + newsletter
